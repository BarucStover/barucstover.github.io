MODEL DESCRIPTION

Mathematical Foundations

The system implements an all-to-all coupled Kuramoto oscillator model using a discrete Euler integration scheme.

Each oscillator has a natural frequency and an evolving phase. Coupling can produce collective synchronization, depending on the frequency distribution and initial conditions.

The displayed spatial network is an illustrative projection of oscillator phases. Its geometry does not represent physical interactions, molecular coordinates, or experimental measurements.

This demonstration is independent of ongoing experimental research and is not a validated biological or ferrofluid model.
